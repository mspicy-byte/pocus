    Optimizing Cardiac POCUS for Residents
    Improving Image Quality and Interpretation Skills

    This training module is designed to help resident physicians refine their cardiac POCUS technique. Learn how to acquire clear images, interpret findings accurately, and apply best practices in clinical settings.

    • Image Acquisition
    • Clinical Interpretation
    • Quality Improvement

    Key Training Focus Areas

    Resident physicians will develop skills in acquiring optimal views, adjusting probe angles, and recognizing normal vs abnormal cardiac structures using POCUS.

    • Image Acquisition

      Learn to position the probe correctly to capture clear and reproducible cardiac views for each standard window.

    • Probe Techniques

      Master probe orientation, depth, and angle adjustments to optimize image clarity in challenging patient scenarios.

    • Interpretation Skills

      Practice analyzing cardiac structures, identifying common pathologies, and differentiating normal variants from disease.

    • Workflow Efficiency

      Integrate POCUS into bedside decision-making effectively, minimizing exam time while maximizing diagnostic value.

    • Quality Feedback

      Receive guidance and feedback to improve image quality and interpretation accuracy for ongoing learning.

    • Continuous Learning

      Track your progress and refine skills through repeated practice and structured training modules.
